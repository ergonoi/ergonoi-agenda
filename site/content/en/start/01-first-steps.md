# First steps

*From the first visit to the first entry.*

## Open the app

Open the address of an agenda server in your browser. If you do not run one yourself, the person who gave you the address runs it. The first page asks how to begin; if you are new, choose *New account*.

## Your eight words

The app shows eight words. They are your journal: whoever has them can read and change it, and nothing else gives access. Write them down on paper and keep them where you keep important documents. The app asks for two of them to make sure.

Nobody can restore lost words, not even the operator of the server. This is the price of a server that cannot read your journal.

Give your journal a name, for example your first name, and tap *Continue*.

## Your PIN

Then choose a PIN of six to twelve digits. This browser keeps your journals, their words and codes only encrypted with it and asks for it when the app opens; showing words or codes asks for it again. On a phone the app shows its own keypad for the PIN; ✓ confirms. On a computer, type it and press Enter. A forgotten PIN is no loss of data: *Forgot PIN* clears this browser, and you set it up again with your eight words.

## The first entry

Type into the field at the top, or dictate, and tap *Add*:

```
Call the plumber
```

The app asks for a facet. Choose one, or create a new one. The calendar and clock buttons in this dialog set a date and time; without them the entry is for today. That is all an entry needs.

## Change and close

- Tap the text of an entry to change it.
- Tap a tag to change or remove it; `+` adds one.
- Swipe an entry to the right to mark it done or move it to another day.

## Example entries

A new account starts with a few example entries and tags. *Settings → Example tags and entries* removes them or keeps them.
