# Version 2

*Owners, allies and guests — and how to upgrade an older journal.*

## What changed

In version 1, the eight words were the only code: whoever shared a journal had all rights, including deleting it. Version 2 separates the levels: the owner keeps the words, allies get a code to read and write, guests a code to read. The server enforces what the keys do not: only the owner can delete the journal or replace it as a whole.

New journals are of version 2. The journal list shows the version and your level for every journal.

## Upgrading a journal

Journals of version 1 that you created can be upgraded: in the journal's dialog, *Version → Upgrade to version 2*. The app copies the journal into a new one of version 2 with new words, and you are its owner. The new words are shown once: write them down.

- The old journal stays on the server and points to the new one. Anyone who opens it with its old words is led to the new journal as a guest and is told so.
- Give the people who should write the ally code of the new journal.
- Once nobody uses the old journal any more, *Delete the version 1 journal on server* removes it.

A journal that someone else created can only be upgraded by them.

## Your default journal on other devices

If you upgrade your default journal, the device you upgrade on uses the new one at once. Your other devices notice the upgrade and ask for the new words in the dialog of the default journal. Until you enter them there, what they write still arrives in the new journal.

## Forgotten words

As long as the old journal is on the server, its old words still lead to the content: the command-line tool prints the old journal and, through it, the new one as a guest, and the lines can be copied into a fresh journal. Once the old journal is deleted, this way back is gone; keep the new words safe.
