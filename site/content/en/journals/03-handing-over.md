# Handing over and ending

*Giving a journal to someone else, ending sharing, deleting.*

## Handing over

A journal you own can be given to someone else: in its dialog, *Ending → Hand over to someone else* shows a handover code. The other person enters it where they add a journal, or, on a new device, under *Take over a journal*. Their app copies the journal under new words of their own; they become its owner, and you stay an ally. Until it is taken over, *Withdraw the handover* cancels it.

## Stop sharing

*Stop sharing for anybody* gives the journal new words: the app moves its content into a new journal, puts it on all your devices and deletes the old one on the server. Everybody else loses access; what they have already seen stays in their browser. Give the new codes to those who should keep using it.

## Delete on server

*Delete on server* removes the journal from the server. Anybody who may write to it keeps a copy in their browser and is offered to restore it. To end all access, use *Stop sharing*.

## Remove from my journals

*Remove from my journals* takes a journal out of your list, on all your devices. The journal itself stays on the server for the others.
