# Several journals

*Your own journal, and the ones you share.*

## Your journal and others

Your account is your own journal, the *default journal*. Further journals can be added: one you create for the family, one a club shares with you, one you only read. The list shows the entries of all of them together; each entry stays in its journal, and entries of other journals carry the journal's name.

## Adding a journal

*Settings → + Add journal*:

- *Create a new journal* creates one with its own eight words. You are its owner.
- To add a journal someone gave you, enter the code you received, or tap *Scan QR code*.

The journals you add belong to your account: your other devices show them after their next sync.

## Which journal an entry goes to

The facet decides. Every facet belongs to one journal; wherever several journals are involved, the facet choice shows the journal's name in small letters after the facet (`family · Home`). Choosing it writes the entry into that journal. To move an entry, tap its facet and choose a facet of the other journal. Nothing moves without your choice, and *Undo* takes a move back.

## Name and alias

A journal names itself with a line `Journal name: …`; everybody who shares it sees this name. In the journal's dialog (*Settings → Journals*, tap *Edit*) you can change it, or give a journal of someone else an *alias* that only you see.

## Your own view of a shared journal

In a shared journal's dialog, *For you only: every entry of this journal also has the facet* gives all its entries one of your own facets, in your views only. Give the journals of your children your facet `family`, and one tap on `family` shows all of them together with your own family entries.

On a single entry of another journal, `+` → *another facet* adds a facet that only you see. It is kept in your own journal and drawn with a dashed border.
