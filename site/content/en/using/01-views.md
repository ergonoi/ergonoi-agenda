# List, month and text

*Three ways to look at the same journal.*

## List

The list groups what is coming: *Today* (with what is overdue), *Tomorrow*, *Next 7 days*, *Later* (up to 30 days). Each group shows a limited number of entries; *more* shows the rest. Entries of other journals carry the journal's name.

- **Swipe right** on an entry: *Done*, or move it by a day, two days, a week, a month, or to a date you choose.
- **Tap the text** to change it, **tap a tag** to change it, **`+`** to add one.
- *Undo* appears for a moment after every change.

## Month

*Month* shows the same entries as a calendar: dots in the facet colours under each day, and below the grid the entries of the day you tap. A series appears on each of its days. With a day chosen, a new entry gets that date.

## Text

*Text* shows the journal as what it is: lines of text, one entry per line. *Manual edit* makes it editable like a text file: change lines, add lines, copy everything. Lines to delete are ticked on the left. Nothing is written until you tap *Save*; lines that cannot be read, such as an impossible date, are named and must be corrected first.

The text view is also the place for lines that describe the journal itself, such as its name (`Journal name: …`) and the definitions of facets (see *Facets and filters*).

## Search

The magnifier at the top (or `/` on a computer) searches text, notes and tags of all journals, beyond the next 30 days. *include done* finds closed entries as well; *Memos* lists the memos. The `×` in the search field clears it.
