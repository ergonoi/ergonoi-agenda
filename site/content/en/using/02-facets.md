# Facets and filters

*Areas of life as colours, and showing only what matters now.*

## Views

A view is a filter with a name: which facets are on, and which other tags are hidden. The title at the top shows the view you are in. Tap the title to switch to another view, or to save the current filter as a new one with *Save current as view …*. Views are kept in your journal and apply on all your devices.

If you change the filter while a view is active, a dot appears after its name, and a line below the input field says what changed, with *Save* to keep the change in the view and *Revert* to go back to it.

## The facet panel

The funnel at the top opens the facet panel; on a wide screen it stands beside the list all the time. It lists your views, then the facets grouped under the journal they belong to. A lock after a journal's name means that you read it as a guest.

Tap a facet to hide or show it; press and hold to show only this one (a message offers *Undo*). Tap a journal's name to switch all its facets; press and hold to show only this journal. *All* shows all facets, *None* hides all of them. Facets that are switched on are dark, facets that are switched off are light. There is nothing in between.

Below the facets, *More* filters by the other tags: topic, action, rank, size, time, repetition. Without a view, a line below the input field names what is hidden, with *Show all*.

## Creating and keeping a facet

A facet exists as soon as an entry uses it. When you create one in the facet choice, the app also writes a line that keeps it even when no entry uses it:

```
Tag definition: family appointments #facet-family #type-meta
```

The text after the colon says what the facet stands for. Such lines never appear in the list; you see them in the *Text* view. Topics and views are kept in the same way.

## Renaming

*Settings → Rename tag* renames a facet or topic in the journals you choose. A preview tells per journal how many entries change; if the new name exists already, both are merged.

## Removing

In a journal's dialog, *Remove a facet from this journal* takes a facet out of that journal. If open entries still carry it, you choose which other facet of the journal they get instead; then the facet's definition is closed. No entry is closed by this.

## The same name in several journals

Two journals may both have a facet `sport`. They are different facets: each stands under its own journal in the facet panel, has its own colour, and when you add an entry the app asks which one you mean. Elsewhere the journal's name follows it (`sport · Anna`).
