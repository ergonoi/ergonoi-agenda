# Facets and filters

*Areas of life as colours, and showing only what matters now.*

## The facet bar

Below the input field, the facet bar shows every facet with its number of entries. Tap a facet to hide or show it; press and hold to show only this one. *All* shows all facets again, *None* hides all of them, so that you can switch on single ones. Facets you use most come first.

Facets that are switched on are dark, facets that are switched off are light. There is nothing in between.

## The filter tab

The funnel at the top filters by the other tags: topic, action, rank, size, time, repetition. The result appears in the list, with a line naming the filter and *Reset*.

## Creating and keeping a facet

A facet exists as soon as an entry uses it. When you create one in the facet choice, the app also writes a line that keeps it even when no entry uses it:

```
Tag definition: family appointments #facet-family #type-meta
```

The text after the colon says what the facet stands for. Such lines never appear in the list; you see them in the *Text* view. Topics are kept in the same way.

## Renaming

*Settings → Rename tag* renames a facet or topic in the journals you choose. A preview tells per journal how many entries change; if the new name exists already, both are merged.

## The same name in several journals

Two journals may both have a facet `sport`. They are different facets: wherever the name occurs more than once, the journal's name follows it (`sport · Anna`), each has its own colour, and when you add an entry the app asks which one you mean. In the facet bar, one button switches the name in all journals; *Facets by journal* in the filter tab chooses single ones.
